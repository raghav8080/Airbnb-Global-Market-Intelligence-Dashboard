# 🌍 Airbnb Global Market Intelligence Dashboard

> An interactive Power BI analytics solution processing **270K+ listings**, **180K+ hosts**, and **5.3M+ reviews** across 10 major global cities to uncover actionable insights into pricing dynamics, market growth, host trust, and guest satisfaction.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
The **Airbnb Global Market Intelligence Dashboard** bridges the gap between raw data and strategic business intelligence. This project models historical market lifecycles, benchmarks global city performance, and surfaces critical metrics regarding host verification, review seasonality, and pricing disparities against traditional hospitality sectors.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Analytical Insights

### 📈 1. Listing Lifecycle & Market Evolution (2008–2020)
* **Lifecycle Modeling:** Mapped Airbnb’s trajectory across six distinct phases: *Introduction, Growth, Maturity, Decline, Reinvention, and COVID-19*.
* **Milestones Identified:** Pinpointed the **2015 global growth peak** and analyzed the post-2017 strategic recovery segmented by room type.

### 🌐 2. Market Concentration & Pareto Analysis
* **Dominant Hubs:** Discovered that **Paris, New York, and Sydney** account for ~48% of total listings and 59% of all platform reviews.
* **Economic Drivers:** Linked Paris’s market dominance to a structural pricing advantage where hotel rooms average roughly **2x the cost** of comparable Airbnb listings.

### 🗺️ 3. City-Level Quality & Satisfaction Heatmap
* **Performance Benchmarks:** Designed a multi-variable heatmap tracking *Accuracy, Cleanliness, Communication, Location, and Value*.
* **Top & Bottom Tiers:** Ranked **Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro** as top-rated destinations, while **Hong Kong and Istanbul** lagged at the bottom.
* **Core Vulnerabilities:** Flagged **Cleanliness** and **Value** as the weakest-performing categories globally across urban markets.

### 👥 4. Host Trust, Seasonality, & Data Hygiene
* **Engagement Patterns:** Uncovered that **86.5% of reviewers posted only once**, highlighting a heavily skewed retention curve.
* **Seasonal Trends:** Identified predictable booking and review surges from **April to August** in European powerhouses like Paris and Rome.
* **Trust & Security:** Evaluated host credentials, noting that **66.9% of hosts** feature full profile verification and pictures.
* **Data Quality Audit:** Successfully identified and flagged an outlier reviewer with **283 reviews** as a potential web-scraping or data-quality anomaly.
