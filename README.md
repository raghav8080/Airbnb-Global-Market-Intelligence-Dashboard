Airbnb Global Market Intelligence Dashboard

Built an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 270K+ Airbnb listings, 180K+ hosts and 5.3M+ reviews across 10 global cities, turning raw data into insights on growth, pricing, ratings and trust.
Modeled Airbnb's 2008-2020 listing lifecycle (introduction, growth, maturity, decline, reinvention, COVID-19) by room type, identifying the 2015 peak and the post-2017 recovery.
Ran a Pareto and market-share analysis, finding that Paris, New York and Sydney make up ~48% of listings and 59% of reviews. Linked Paris's dominance to a pricing gap where hotel rooms cost roughly 2x Airbnb rates.
Designed a city-level ratings heatmap across accuracy, cleanliness, communication, location and value. It showed Mexico City and Rio as top-rated, Hong Kong and Istanbul as lowest, and cleanliness and value as the weakest categories.
Analyzed review frequency, seasonality and host trust signals: 86.5% of reviewers posted only once, Paris and Rome peak from April to August, and 66.9% of hosts are verified with a profile picture. Also flagged an outlier reviewer (283 reviews) as a possible data-quality issue.
